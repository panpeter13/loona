Privacy Policy — English

LOONA (operator: PAN PETR) processes only the information needed to identify an account, store cycle dates, calculate estimates, deliver reminders, and respond to errors.

Stored data includes a one-way hash of the platform user identifier, language, time zone, cycle settings, cycle start and end dates, and notification history. Separate consent is obtained before health-related cycle data is recorded.

Data is retained until the user deletes the account, except where retention is required by law. Database hosting is provided by Supabase, Inc. in Singapore (Southeast Asia, ap-southeast-1) using encrypted network transmission. LOONA does not sell personal information.

Users may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing. The service is not intended for children under 14 until a parental-consent procedure is available.
